Create cloze flashcards

On a cloze card a word is missing from the sentence. You have to know it, not just recognise it.

How to write them

When creating a card, tap the word you are after (or select it) and press “Blank”. Or put it in double curly braces yourself:

The capital of France is {{Paris}}.

The front shows a blank. On the back you see the full sentence.

Several blanks

A sentence may have several blanks, for example {{Paris}} lies on the {{Seine}}.

Good cloze cards

  • Hide only one key word, not half the sentence.
  • A whole sentence sticks better than a single word.
  • Examples: dates in history, technical terms in biology, formulas in physics.

Tap the words

With an account there is the word bank: below the card lie the words you need plus two wrong ones. You tap them in order. That is easier than typing and suits children.

Type the blank

With an account you can also type the words. KarteiDeck checks the input. Capitalisation, accents and small typos do not count as wrong.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an answer of my own?

No. While studying, KarteiDeck shows green or red at once.

How do I turn a sentence into a cloze text?

Write the sentence, tap or select the word and press “Blank”. “The moon orbits the Earth.” becomes “The {{moon}} orbits the Earth.” You do not need an answer of your own.
