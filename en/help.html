Help and frequently asked questions

Studying

How do I study?

Tap a card in the fan, “Show answer”, then “Got it” or “Again”. Cards you got right come back later, hard ones sooner.

What does “Skip” do?

The card goes to the tray at the top left and gets a blue tab. A click on the tray brings it back.

What are markers and the pin?

Pink means Again, blue means Later. With the pin (key P) you pin a card. The “Pinned” mode only practises these cards.

Which study modes are there?

Reverse, type the answer, word bank for cloze text, reading aloud and an exam with a grade. You find them under Options, Study modes (with an account).

Which keys are there?

Space flips the card, right arrow means Got it, left arrow Again. P pins, Esc puts back, 1 to 7 picks a card.

What does Progress show?

In the “Progress” tab (with an account) you switch between day, month and year. You see how many cards you studied, sorted by category, how many you got right and which ones newly reached the top box. In the month view every study day gets a stamp.

Do I need an answer of my own?

No. While studying, KarteiDeck shows green or red at once.

How do I turn a sentence into a cloze text?

Write the sentence, tap or select the word and press “Blank”. “The moon orbits the Earth.” becomes “The {{moon}} orbits the Earth.” You do not need an answer of your own.

Cards and saving

How do I load cards from Anki, Quizlet or CSV?

In the “Cards” tab tap “Load cards”. KarteiDeck reads CSV, the Anki text export (Notes in Plain Text) and the Quizlet export. Duplicates are skipped.

How do I use cards from other people?

Save the file, then in the “Cards” tab tap “Load cards”. The cards are added, your own stay.

How do I share cards?

In the “Cards” tab tap “Share”. On a phone you choose email or Discord, on a computer a file is downloaded. Your progress stays with you.

Can I use images and tags?

Yes. When creating a card you add an image to the question, also by pasting. Tags and search are in the “Cards” tab. “Table (CSV)” saves everything for Excel or other programs.

How do categories get colours?

When you create a category you pick a colour. In the “Categories” tab you change it with “Edit”. It appears as a dot and as a stripe at the top of the card.

Where are my cards stored?

In the browser on your device. In Chrome or Edge on a computer you can pick a folder under Options, and the page then saves there after every change.

How do I back up my cards?

In the “Cards” tab tap “Save cards”. You keep the file yourself. With “Load cards” you bring it back.

Offline, account and privacy

Do I need internet or a server?

Not after the first load. The page runs in the browser and sends nothing while you study.

Can I install the app?

Yes, via the address bar (“Install”) or on an iPhone via Share and “Add to Home Screen”.

How do I move to another device?

With an account your cards are available on every device. Without an account you save the cards, bring the file to the other device and load it there.

What stays with me, what goes to a server?

Without an account everything stays on your device. There is no tracking, no cookies and no externally loaded fonts or scripts.

Is there a demo?

Yes. The “Demo” switch at the top shows 10 sample cards for grades 7 to 10. They do not end up in your cards.
