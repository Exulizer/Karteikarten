Study flashcards offline

After the first visit your browser remembers the page. From then on it opens without a network too: on the train, on a plane or in the library.

Install it as an app

On a computer, Chrome or Edge shows “Install” in the address bar. On an iPhone: Share, then “Add to Home Screen”. The app opens in its own window.

What works without a network

  • Writing, editing and studying cards
  • Categories, search, light and dark
  • Reading aloud with your device’s voice (with an account)

Typical cases

Studying on the train, in flight mode on a plane, in a library without Wi-Fi: you open KarteiDeck as usual. The cards are in the browser, nothing is loaded. All that matters is that you have opened the page once while online.

Does it really work?

Test it beforehand: open the page, switch on flight mode, reload the page. If your cards appear, you are ready.

What needs a network

  • Logging in and syncing with the account
  • The check for a new version (can be switched off in the options)

Without an account the cards only live in your browser. Back them up with “Save cards”.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need internet or a server?

Not after the first load. The page runs in the browser and sends nothing while you study.

Can I install the app?

Yes, via the address bar (“Install”) or on an iPhone via Share and “Add to Home Screen”.

How do I move to another device?

With an account your cards are available on every device. Without an account you save the cards, bring the file to the other device and load it there.
