Flashcards online, free and without sign-up

KarteiDeck is a flashcard app for the browser. Question on the front, answer on the back, just like at the desk. You need no account, it shows no ads and it runs offline.

How it works

  1. Write a card. Question on the front, answer on the back, with an image if you like.
  2. Flip it. Turn the card over and say honestly: Got it or Again.
  3. Repeat. The card box (Leitner system) shows you cards again when it is time.

What is inside

  • Card box with boxes, categories with colours, tags and search
  • Cloze text and images on cards
  • Import from CSV, Anki and Quizlet
  • Daily goal, streak, light and dark theme
  • Works offline and can be installed as an app
  • With a free account: progress by day, month and year, typing the answer, word bank, reading aloud, exams, cassette player, and your cards on every device

Your data

Without an account the cards stay in your browser on your device. No tracking, no cookies without an account, no external fonts. More in Flashcards without sign-up.
