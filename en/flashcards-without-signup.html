Create flashcards online, no sign-up

You can create flashcards online without signing up. You need no account and no email address: open the page, write a card, study. KarteiDeck is free and shows no ads.

How to create flashcards online

  1. Tap “New” and enter a question and an answer.
  2. Under “Study”, reveal the card and answer with “Got it” or “Again”.
  3. The card box shows the card again later.

Where do the cards stay?

In your browser’s storage, on your device. They are not sent to a server. If you clear the website data, the cards are gone. So save them now and then with “Save cards”.

Example: a card in 20 seconds

Front: “Capital of Australia?” Back: “Canberra”. Save, done. Ten cards like this are enough for the first round. With “Demo” you can first look at 10 sample cards.

What you get without an account

  • Card box with boxes (Leitner system)
  • Cloze text, images, tags and categories
  • Import from CSV, Anki and Quizlet
  • Studying offline too

When is an account worth it?

With the free account your cards and options are available on every device. You also get progress by day, month and year, typing the answer, reading aloud, exams and the cassette player.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my cards stored?

In the browser on your device. In Chrome or Edge on a computer you can pick a folder under Options, and the page then saves there after every change.

How do I back up my cards?

In the “Cards” tab tap “Save cards”. You keep the file yourself. With “Load cards” you bring it back.

What stays with me, what goes to a server?

Without an account everything stays on your device. There is no tracking, no cookies and no externally loaded fonts or scripts.

Is there a demo?

Yes. The “Demo” switch at the top shows 10 sample cards for grades 7 to 10. They do not end up in your cards.
