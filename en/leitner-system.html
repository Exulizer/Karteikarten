Leitner system: the flashcard box explained

The card box is a learning method with boxes. Sebastian Leitner described it in 1972 in his book “So lernt man lernen” (“How to learn to learn”).

The rule

  • New cards start in box 1.
  • Got it: the card moves up one box.
  • Missed: the card goes back to box 1.
  • Cards in higher boxes come up less often.

Why it helps

You practise often what does not stick yet, and rarely what you know. Before turning the card you pull the answer out of your head. This is called active recall.

Example with three cards

You study the cards “dog”, “cat” and “horse”. You know “dog” and “cat”, they move to box 2. You do not know “horse”, the card stays in box 1. Tomorrow “horse” comes up again, “dog” and “cat” only later. So the gap grows with every hit.

How many boxes?

Classically there are five boxes. In KarteiDeck you see the status in the card box, and the tab “Due” shows what is up today. More in the article Learning vocabulary.

In KarteiDeck

Cards you got right move up a box and come back later. “Again” sends them back to box 1. The tab “Due” shows what is up today.

Frequently asked questions

How do I study?

Tap a card in the fan, “Show answer”, then “Got it” or “Again”. Cards you got right come back later, hard ones sooner.

What does “Skip” do?

The card goes to the tray at the top left and gets a blue tab. A click on the tray brings it back.

What are markers and the pin?

Pink means Again, blue means Later. With the pin (key P) you pin a card. The “Pinned” mode only practises these cards.

Which study modes are there?

Reverse, type the answer, word bank for cloze text, reading aloud and an exam with a grade. You find them under Options, Study modes (with an account).

Which keys are there?

Space flips the card, right arrow means Got it, left arrow Again. P pins, Esc puts back, 1 to 7 picks a card.

What does Progress show?

In the “Progress” tab (with an account) you switch between day, month and year. You see how many cards you studied, sorted by category, how many you got right and which ones newly reached the top box. In the month view every study day gets a stamp.
