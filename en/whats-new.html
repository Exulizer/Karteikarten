What’s new

Version 1.7.0New 22 November 2026

  • Safer saving: When you close the tab, the app still saves even large accounts quickly. If far fewer cards show up than in your account, it asks before deleting anything.
  • Progress: You see what is due in the next 7 days and your hardest cards. Tap one to edit it.
  • Progress over the year: Every day is a square, the darker, the more you practised.
  • Streak: One free day per week no longer breaks your streak.
  • Sign in: If your address is not confirmed yet, you can have the mail sent again right there.
  • Account: The buttons now sit neatly side by side. At the top you see when it last synced, for example "Synced automatically at 18:20".
  • Fixed: In rare cases settings and sync data could end up in the same place. This is repaired, existing settings are restored on start.
  • The app loads faster: The English texts are only loaded when you use English.

Version 1.6.0 20 November 2026

  • Profile pictures: 10 pictures, from Newcomer to Legend. Your picture changes by itself when you reach a goal, and a note tells you why.
  • The first levels take a few days. The last ones take months. The highest levels count a share of your cards (up to 80 %), so small decks work too.
  • You see your picture in Progress, next to the account button and in the account window. Levels you reached stay.
  • Account sync: If you practised on two devices, the app merges both without asking. Nothing is lost.

Version 1.5.0 20 November 2026

  • Account redesigned: log in, sign up and account now look like an index card, with tabs, ruled lines, punch holes and a “Logged in” stamp.
  • Fields sit on the lines and fill with a blue line when you tap them.

Version 1.4.1 20 November 2026

  • Easier to read: fields, buttons and switches have a clearer border.
  • Progress by category: “known” is now blue, “again” orange with stripes. Easy to tell apart with red-green colour blindness too.
  • Warnings on cards and in type mode are easier to read.

Version 1.4.0 20 November 2026

  • Phone: the bar has five tabs. Only the active one shows its name, and the tap targets are bigger. Categories sit in a switch inside the Cards tab.
  • Language button at the top, next to “Demo”. Switching keeps you on the page you are on.
  • Progress: “Watch demo” is also there when you have your own data, “End demo” takes you back.
  • Options: the group headings stand out.
  • New “What’s new” page.

Version 1.3.0 19 November 2026

  • New with an account: a “Progress” tab replaces the statistics. Day, month and year, a daily goal with a seal, a calendar with stamps.
  • For each category you see how many cards you studied, how often you got them right and which ones you mastered.
  • Without an account the tab shows an info card, with an empty account a 2-minute demo.
  • KarteiDeck is now fully available in English: app, topic pages, help, legal notice and privacy.

Version 1.2.0 11 November 2026

  • Cloze cards: a “Gap” button when you create or edit a card. With an account there is also a word bank to tap.
  • Import checks the file format before reading (CSV, TSV, TXT, JSON up to 5 MB).
  • Very large screens: the sheet, cards and extras scale up.
  • A smaller page thanks to minified code.

Version 1.1.0

  • New name KarteiDeck with its own logo.
  • Cloze cards, typed answers, reverse study, exam with grade, read aloud.
  • Images, tags and search. Import from CSV, Anki and Quizlet, export and print.
  • Categories with their own colours.
