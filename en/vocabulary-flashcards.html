Learn vocabulary with flashcards

Word on the front, translation on the back. A good vocabulary card needs nothing more.

Tips for better cards

  • One word per card.
  • A short example sentence helps more than a long list. As a cloze text it becomes practice.
  • A category of its own, with its own colour, for each language.

Routine for new vocabulary

  1. Create 10 to 15 new words per study session.
  2. Study briefly every day. The card box shows the words at the right time.
  3. After a week switch direction and quiz the translation.

Cards from other programs

If you already have lists in Anki, Quizlet or Excel, load them as a file. Duplicate cards are skipped. This works without signing up too. See Flashcards without sign-up.

What KarteiDeck offers for this

  • Import from CSV, Anki and Quizlet
  • Reverse or mixed direction, so you practise both ways (with an account)
  • Reading aloud with your device voice in German, English, French, Spanish and Italian (with an account)
  • Typing the answer instead of just flipping (with an account)

Frequently asked questions

How do I load cards from Anki, Quizlet or CSV?

In the “Cards” tab tap “Load cards”. KarteiDeck reads CSV, the Anki text export (Notes in Plain Text) and the Quizlet export. Duplicates are skipped.

How do I use cards from other people?

Save the file, then in the “Cards” tab tap “Load cards”. The cards are added, your own stay.

How do I share cards?

In the “Cards” tab tap “Share”. On a phone you choose email or Discord, on a computer a file is downloaded. Your progress stays with you.

Can I use images and tags?

Yes. When creating a card you add an image to the question, also by pasting. Tags and search are in the “Cards” tab. “Table (CSV)” saves everything for Excel or other programs.

How do categories get colours?

When you create a category you pick a colour. In the “Categories” tab you change it with “Edit”. It appears as a dot and as a stripe at the top of the card.
